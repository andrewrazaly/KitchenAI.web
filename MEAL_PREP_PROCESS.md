# Full Weekly Meal Prep Process

## Overview
A comprehensive 9-step system for efficient weekly meal preparation, designed to save time, reduce food waste, and maintain nutritional goals.

## Process Steps

### 1. Goal Setting (Optional but Powerful)
- **Define your goals**: macros/calories, cost-saving, time-saving, variety, or simplicity
- **Decide meal scope**: how many meals you're prepping for (e.g., all dinners, just lunches, etc.)
- **Set weekly targets**: budget, nutrition goals, time constraints

### 2. Inspiration + Recipe Selection
- **Browse sources**: Instagram, Pinterest, saved Reels, cookbooks, or apps
- **Pick 4–6 core recipes** that share ingredients (saves money and food waste)
- **Consider variety**: different proteins, cooking methods, and cuisines
- **Check difficulty**: balance complex and simple recipes

### 3. Inventory Check (Pantry + Fridge Audit)
- **Check existing stock**: avoid duplicates and reduce waste
- **Cross-reference recipes**: see what you already have
- **Note expiration dates**: prioritize ingredients that need to be used soon
- **Create "use first" list**: items approaching expiration

### 4. Smart Grocery List Creation
- **Auto-categorize by section**:
  - Produce
  - Meat & Seafood
  - Pantry Items
  - Dairy & Eggs
  - Freezer Items
  - Condiments & Spices
- **Combine quantities**: from multiple recipes to avoid overbuying
- **Tag preferences**: discounted or preferred brands if budget-conscious
- **Add backup items**: quick meal options for busy days

### 5. Grocery Shopping
- **Use pre-built list**: digital or print format
- **Shop strategically**: follow store layout to save time
- **Check quality**: especially for produce and proteins
- **Optional enhancements**:
  - Order online for convenience
  - Use AI agents for best local discounts
  - Compare prices across stores

### 6. Prep Day / Batch Cook Day
Choose 1–2 days per week for meal preparation:

#### Full Cook Method
- Cook all meals completely and store in containers
- Best for: busy schedules, grab-and-go convenience

#### Partial Prep Method
- **Chop vegetables**: store in airtight containers
- **Marinate proteins**: prepare for quick cooking later
- **Cook grains/starches**: rice, quinoa, pasta in bulk
- **Pre-cook proteins**: chicken, ground meat, etc.

#### Organization Tips
- **Label containers**: meal name + preparation date
- **Use proper storage**: microwave/oven safe, BPA-free containers
- **Separate components**: keep sauces separate to avoid sogginess

### 7. Meal Planning Calendar
- **Schedule meals by day** based on:
  - **Freshness priority**: fish early week, pasta later
  - **Time availability**: quick meals on busy days
  - **Variety balance**: different cuisines throughout week
- **Integration options**:
  - Sync with calendar app
  - Use dedicated meal planner
  - Create visual meal board

### 8. Storage & Portioning
- **Container selection**: microwave/oven safe, BPA-free, various sizes
- **Proper portioning**: based on nutritional goals and appetite
- **Sauce separation**: prevent sogginess and maintain texture
- **Freezer strategy**: freeze overflow meals for backup weeks
- **Labeling system**: clear dates and contents for easy identification

### 9. Feedback & Adjust
End-of-week evaluation process:
- **What worked well?**: successful recipes and processes
- **What didn't work?**: identify pain points and failures
- **Recipe tracking**: maintain journal or app of liked meals
- **Build rotation list**: catalog of reliable go-to recipes
- **Process refinement**: adjust timing, quantities, or methods
